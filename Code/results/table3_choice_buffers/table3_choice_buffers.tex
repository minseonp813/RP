\textbf{Buffer definitions.} Let $q=x/(x+y)$ be the payoff share. With buffer $b$, a corner choice satisfies $q\leq b$ or $q\geq 1-b$, and an equal-allocation choice satisfies $|q-0.5|\leq b$. Each control is the fraction of a member's 18 choices meeting the definition. As in the current table, regressions include the member's share and its signed difference from the partner's share, separately for corners and equal allocations.

Exact equality is very restrictive: only 0.61\% of choices satisfy $x=y$, compared with 26.50\% within the 2.5-point band. Despite this substantial change in classification, the CCEI coefficients remain stable across the three alternatives.

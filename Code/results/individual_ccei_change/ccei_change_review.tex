\emph{Notes}: The dependent variable is the change in individual CCEI from baseline to endline. Each column reports an individual-level OLS regression using the 1,256 students in the balanced sample of Table~3, with one observation per student. Column (1) includes baseline gender, height, math score, the five personality measures, and friendship-network out-degree and in-degree. Column (2) additionally includes endline-minus-baseline changes in math score, out-degree, and in-degree. Neither specification includes class fixed effects, partner characteristics, within-pair differences, risk aversion, or choice-pattern shares. Missing baseline math and personality values are set to zero, with corresponding missing-value indicators included; their coefficients and the constant are omitted from the display. Standard errors are clustered across 64 classes and reported in parentheses. +, *, and ** denote significance at the 10\%, 5\%, and 1\% levels, respectively.

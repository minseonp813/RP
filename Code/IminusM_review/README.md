# Review package: placebo-adjusted distance as the main outcome

This directory is an isolated analysis workspace for comparing three outcome
definitions without changing the manuscript or its current tables and figures:

1. the observed distance, `I`;
2. the placebo-adjusted distance, `I - M`;
3. the observed distance controlling flexibly for `M`.

All generated files are written under `outputs/`. The scripts read the existing
replication data and benchmark files but do not overwrite them.

## Benchmark calculation after integration (2026-10-06)

The production entry point is now `../1_1_calculate_indices.R`, section 7. It
calls the single shared `../programs/build_placebo_donor_matrices.R` for CCEI,
HM, MaxMPI and risk aversion, using the balanced pair roster, actual indices
and choice data from `1_1_calculate_indices.R`. New full outputs go to `../results/benchmarks/` and
the benchmark columns are carried through `2_1_build_panel.R`, `2_2_build_panel_group.R`,
and `2_3_build_panel_individual.R`. Section 7 can resume
from the actual-index checkpoint without recalculating the preceding indices.

The pre-integration scripts and panel files are preserved locally in
`../Archive/M_before_integration_2026-10-06/`. Existing benchmark outputs and
manuscript results remain in their original locations for comparison. The
review analyses below still consume those retained inputs; switching their
tables and figures to the new full benchmarks is a subsequent step.

The redundant review launchers 01 and 04 and historical pilot launcher 04a
were removed on 2026-10-06; copies are retained locally in
`../Archive/draft_figures_A7_4_2026-10-06/Code/IminusM_review/`.
Main `1_1_calculate_indices.R` is the entry point for all full benchmark calculations.
`02_ccei_ra_analysis.do` still reads the retained
`outputs/data/ra_placebo_member_wave.dta`. The old caches have no input/config
identity and cannot be resumed by the new builder. No full calculation was
run during integration or the manuscript figure update.

## Historical review execution order

This sequence documents the original review results, rather than an active
pipeline. The benchmark launchers are now archived; use main
`1_1_calculate_indices.R` for new full
calculations and retain the old outputs when comparing specifications.

1. Archived review 01 (RA benchmark)
2. `02_ccei_ra_analysis.do`
3. `03_review_outputs.R`
4. Archived review 04a (50-donor HM and 20-donor MaxMPI pilot builds)
5. `05_alternative_measure_analysis.do`
6. `07_hm_sample_stability.R`
7. `08_hm_sample_stability.do`

The retained review builds used 50 reproducibly sampled HM donors and 20
reproducibly sampled MaxMPI donors per target and include split-half stability
checks. Exact full HM and MaxMPI builds now belong to main `1_1_calculate_indices.R`; each requires
850,208 target-donor comparisons, and some exact cross-cost problems are
computationally intensive.

For the MaxMPI review build, an individual cross-cost calculation that does not
finish within two seconds is recorded as undefined and receives the neutral
distance of one half. The generated diagnostics report the resulting undefined
share. This cap is only for deciding whether the specification is worth a full
exact build.

`outputs/benchmarks/hm/` contains the first resumable chunk of the exact
651-donor HM build. `outputs/checkpoints/` retains discarded uncapped MaxMPI
review chunks for provenance; none of those partial files enter the reported
results.

## Main/appendix figure migration (2026-10-06)

Current Figure 3 and the revised Figure 4 are generated by
`../07_Figures_Main.R`. Figure 4 uses one uniformly sampled non-own donor
from all 651 same-wave donors per pair-wave (seed 20260812). Both members
share the donor, undefined donor distances receive 0.5, and plotted targets
are restricted to the same 2,560 defined-actual-distance student-waves as
Figure 3. Its outcome is the donor distance minus the same full benchmark M.
The retained full donor matrix is reused; no regressions or index builds run.

Figure A7's calculation is now the independently runnable Figure A7 section
of `../08_Tables_Appendix.do`, and its two panels are drawn by
`../09_Figures_Appendix.R`. Individual fixed effects enter first. Panel (a)
allocates overall explained variation between individual means and additional
within-individual fit; Panel (b) decomposes conditional within R-squared over
the four covariate blocks, including M and excluding RA. It matches Table 3
Column (3), N=2,512 and 64 classes. The earlier FE-as-a-Shapley-player
decomposition in review 02 remains a historical comparison, not Figure A7's input.

The old review plotter 11 was archived under
`../Archive/figures_A7_4_before_migration_2026-10-06/`; its unchanged Figure A6
histogram calculation also moved to 09. Canonical figures are written to
`../results/figures/`; the review exporter copies Figures 3, 4, A6 and A7
from there. Figures 4 and A7 were added to the manuscript on 2026-10-06,
with revised captions, notes and related result text.

Review launchers 01 and 04 duplicated the full production builds in main
`1_1_calculate_indices.R`;
04a reproduced historical sampled/capped pilot builds. These launchers have
been removed from active code. The old benchmark
outputs remain necessary until full production benchmarks are calculated
and downstream data updated. No full benchmark build was run here.
